In short

Tru Photo Books is a wedding photobook made in Egypt: an A5 hardcover (14.8 × 21 cm) with 26 or 50 pages of 200 gsm archival matte paper, printed and bound in Cairo. Books are delivered anywhere in Egypt or picked up at a Tru booth. To order one, message Tru Booth on WhatsApp or send the inquiry form.

A hardcover wedding photobook, made to be kept

Most photos from a wedding end up on phones, scrolled past and almost never printed. A book is different. It sits on a shelf, comes out when family visits, and gets passed from hand to hand.

Tru Photo Books is our answer to that: a compact A5 hardcover, printed and bound in Cairo on archival matte paper. It is small enough to hold in one hand and made to stay on the shelf.

A photo book made in Cairo: size, pages and paper

A5 hardcover
14.8 × 21 cm, a compact size that sits easily on a shelf or a coffee table, with a hard cover that protects the pages.
26 or 50 pages
Choose 26 pages for a tight edit of the night, or 50 for room to tell more of it.
200 gsm archival matte paper
Heavy pages with a matte finish that keeps glare down, chosen for a book you mean to keep.
Printed and bound in Cairo
Every book is printed and bound in Cairo, then delivered across Egypt or picked up at a Tru booth.
